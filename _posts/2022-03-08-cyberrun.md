The first project you make at The Game Assembly is an endless runner style game. The game is very similar to Subway Surfers, and lets the player choose between three different lanes and dodge objects by jumping or sliding.

- Project name: Endless runner
- Period: 25 Jan - 8 Mar
- Speed: 50%
- Group name: P1G5
- Engine: Unity
- Language: C#

## My area

On this project I worked on the movement mechanic for the game and handling of collisions. I also took part in instructing team members who were inexperienced in Unity, as I had previous experience with it. I solved technical performance issues and spent time optimizing them as well as I could. Looking back now 1.5+ years later, there were many obvious fixes that we missed to improve the performance by a lot.
